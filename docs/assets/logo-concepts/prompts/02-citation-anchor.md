Concept direction: evidence and citation return.

Create a square app-icon logo mark showing a selected text passage connected to a small citation anchor node. The mark should imply "from this passage to evidence elsewhere in the book, then back to the original text." Use a thin muted teal line or loop from a selected line to a small rounded citation marker. Add one tiny warm spark at the selected passage as the understanding trigger.

Composition:
- Warm ivory paper background.
- Ink-charcoal page/text-line shapes.
- Muted teal selection and citation connector.
- One small golden spark only.
- The connector should be minimal, not a network graph.
