Design a polished logo mark for "Spark 引读", an AI deep-reading desktop app.

Brand idea:
- The product helps serious readers understand a selected passage more deeply.
- The user starts by selecting text in a PDF.
- AI uses that passage as an anchor, searches the whole book for evidence, and returns clickable citations.
- It is not a chatbot, not a summary tool, and not an AI magic product.

Visual tone:
- Quiet, trustworthy, paper-like, restrained, premium desktop app icon.
- Warm ivory paper, ink charcoal, muted teal accent, tiny warm golden spark only as a secondary detail.
- Minimal geometric mark, strong silhouette, legible at small app-icon sizes.
- Centered composition, balanced negative space, professional vector-logo feel rendered as a high-quality bitmap.

Hard constraints:
- No robot, no chat bubble, no magic wand, no flame, no cyberpunk purple-orange gradient, no complex knowledge graph.
- No readable text, no wordmark, no UI screenshot, no mockup, no photographic scene.
- Do not fill the image with sparkles; use at most one small spark point.
- Keep it simple enough to become a macOS app icon and SVG logo later.
