Concept direction: selected passage as the primary symbol.

Create a square app-icon logo mark showing a simplified warm paper page with three or four subtle horizontal text lines. One central line is surrounded by a precise thin selection frame / corner brackets in muted teal. A single tiny warm spark appears at one corner of the selection frame, suggesting understanding being lit up from the original text. The page shape should be abstract and elegant, not literal document clutter.

Composition:
- Rounded-square app icon background in warm ivory.
- Centered page or book-page glyph.
- Selection frame is the most recognizable feature.
- Small spark is secondary and restrained.
- Use subtle depth/shadow only if it improves app-icon polish.
